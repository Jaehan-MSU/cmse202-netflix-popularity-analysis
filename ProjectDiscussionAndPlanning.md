## Project Goal
Our goal is to analyze Netflix movies and TV shows using real data. We want to compare movies and TV shows, find popular titles, and see how likes, views, and ratings are related to popularity.

## Communication Plan
Our group will use text messages or a group chat for quick communication. We will use GitHub and shared documents to share files, code, notes, and planning information.

## Meeting Plan
We will meet once a week for about 30 to 60 minutes. If someone cannot attend a meeting, they should tell the group early and check the meeting notes afterward.

## Contribution Plan
We will divide the project into clear parts so everyone knows what they are responsible for. We will use GitHub commits, shared documents, and weekly check-ins to make sure everyone is contributing. If someone gets stuck, they should ask the group for help early.
